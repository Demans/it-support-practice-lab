# KB-002 — External monitor not detected

## Quick checks
1. Confirm monitor power and the selected input.
2. Reseat cables at both ends.
3. Test display detection in the operating system.
4. Isolate the cable, adapter, dock, port, and monitor with known-good lab parts.
5. Power-cycle the dock and display.
6. Check approved driver and firmware updates.

## Escalate when
- The port shows physical damage.
- Multiple known-good displays fail on the same device.
- Firmware or driver deployment requires elevated access.
- Evidence suggests dock or graphics hardware failure.

Never publish real asset tags, serial numbers, office locations, or private screenshots.
